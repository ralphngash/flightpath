About Us

The Flight Path Team

We’re a tight-knit group of friends and former co-workers who wanted to build a great bar for the Burien neighborhood. Dan, the owner, brings eighteen years of restaurant-opening and managing experience — and a team that’s ready to serve you.

Come for a lively, welcoming room. Catch up with friends, meet new ones, and stay for the games between rounds.
